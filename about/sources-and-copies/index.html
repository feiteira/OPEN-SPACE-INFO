    Sources & copies¶

    In one sentence

    All information on this site was retrieved from sources publicly available online. This page links back to every one of them.

    How the information was gathered¶

    Every document and dataset listed below was downloaded from a public web location that needed no login, payment or circumvention of access controls. Each download was logged with its original URL, the UTC time of the copy and a SHA-256 checksum. Three files that had moved were retrieved from Internet Archive snapshots of their original addresses; their entries say so. Text was extracted from the PDFs, and every wiki page cites the clause and PDF page it relies on. Pages that analyse the Solar Orbiter MIB cite the release they queried. The JPL Horizons ephemeris used by the Solar Orbiter simulator consists of API responses, kept unchanged in one zip file together with a manifest of each query URL, download time and checksum.

    Copies of the files are kept unchanged in this repository's third-party/ folder, so the citations stay checkable if an original location disappears. They remain the property of their owners and are not covered by the MIT License of the wiki text and code. If you are a rights holder and want a file removed, please open an issue on the repository.

    Not copied¶

    • Five AIAA SpaceOps papers (SCOS-2000 Release 5, NIS, Data Security, Multi-Spacecraft, Common EGSE/MCS for Herschel/Planck) returned HTTP 403 (paywall). They are cited by title only.
    • The Herschel/Planck spacecraft database zips in the ESA Herschel Science Archive (/hsa/legacy/operations/moc/sdb/data/) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-05 and again on 2026-11-12.
    • Two Herschel flight-operations ground-segment procedures (C_GSP_MCS_SCOS and C_GSP_MCS_NCTRS) are cited and listed below, but their PDFs are not hosted here because they print operational login credentials.
    • 109 of the 111 Solar Orbiter MIB releases on the IRAP server: only the first (2020-04-16) and the latest (2025-09-16) were copied, to avoid a long bulk download from a small team server.
