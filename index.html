    Spacecraft telemetry & telecommand, explained from the sources

    How spacecraft talk to the ground¶

    One open wiki covers the whole chain, from a bit in an on-board packet to a value on an operator's screen. It explains the CCSDS protocols, the ECSS Packet Utilization Standard, Space Link Extension, ESA's SCOS-2000 mission control system and its Mission Information Base. Every statement cites the document and page it comes from.
