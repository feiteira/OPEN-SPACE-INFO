## Summary

A 12-page set of tree charts showing the whole ECSS document system, published by ECSS in November 2025. The first chart shows the system description ECSS-S-ST-00, the glossary ECSS-S-ST-00-01 and the five branches with their disciplines: space project management (M), product assurance (Q), engineering (E), sustainability (U) and industrialization, production and maintenance (I). The following charts list every standard per discipline for the management, industrialization, engineering (with separate E-30 mechanical and E-50 communications charts), product assurance (with a separate Q-70 materials chart) and sustainability branches. They are followed by the handbooks and technical memoranda of the PA and engineering branches, and the ECSS policy (P-00) and documentation (D-00) series.

For this wiki, two charts matter most. The E-70 'Ground systems and operations' column lists ECSS-E-ST-70C, -70-01C (on-board control procedures), -70-11C Rev.1 (space segment operability), -70-31C (monitoring and control data definition), -70-32C (test and operations procedure language), and -70-41C with its Rev.1 marked as under update. The E-50 'Communications' chart lists ECSS-E-ST-50C Rev.2, the ranging, RF and on-board bus standards, and the CCSDS adoption notices ECSS-E-AS-50-21C Rev.2 to -26C (TM coding, TM SDLP, AOS, TC coding, TC SDLP, COP-1). The older ECSS-E-ST-50-01C, -50-03C and -50-04C do not appear in that chart. Handbook ECSS-E-HB-50A appears under E-50.

The charts are useful for placing the standards covered on [ECSS](../ecss/index.md), [E-50 communications](../ecss/e-st-50-communications.md), [E-ST-70C](../ecss/e-st-70c.md) and the [standards map](../overview/standards-map.md) in their ECSS context. They are an index only and contain no technical content.


## Key points

- ECSS has five branches: space project management (M-10, M-40, M-60, M-80), space product assurance (Q-10 to Q-80), space engineering (E-10 to E-80), space sustainability (U-10 space debris, U-20 planetary protection) and industrialization, production and maintenance (I-10 to I-30). They sit under ECSS-S-ST-00 (system description) and ECSS-S-ST-00-01 (glossary of terms). *(PDF p. 1)*
- E-50 is 'Communications', E-70 is 'Ground systems and operations' and E-80 is 'Security'. *(PDF p. 1)*
- The E-70 column lists ECSS-E-ST-70C (Ground systems and operations), ECSS-E-ST-70-01C (On-board control procedures), ECSS-E-ST-70-11C Rev.1 (Space segment operability), ECSS-E-ST-70-31C (Monitoring and control data definition), ECSS-E-ST-70-32C (Test and operations procedure language) and ECSS-E-ST-70-41C (Telemetry and telecommand packet utilization), with ECSS-E-ST-70-41C Rev.1 shown alongside a 'U' (ongoing update) marker. *(PDF p. 4)*
- The engineering branch chart also lists ECSS-E-ST-80C (Security in space systems lifecycles) under E-80 and ECSS-E-ST-40C Rev.1 (Software general requirements) under E-40. *(PDF p. 4)*
- The E-50 chart lists ECSS-E-ST-50C Rev.2 (Communications), ECSS-E-ST-50-02C (Ranging and Doppler tracking) and ECSS-E-ST-50-05C Rev.2 (Radio frequency and modulation), plus the on-board link and bus standards: -50-11C SpaceFibre, -50-12C Rev.1 SpaceWire, -50-13C MIL-STD-1553B, -50-14C discrete interfaces, -50-15C CAN bus, -50-16 TTE, and -50-51C/-52C/-53C SpaceWire protocols. *(PDF p. 6)*
- The E-50 chart lists six CCSDS adoption notices: ECSS-E-AS-50-21C Rev.2 (CCSDS 131.0-B-5 TM synchronization and channel coding), -22C Rev.1 (132.0-B-3 TM SDLP), -23C Rev.1 (732.0-B-4 AOS SDLP), -24C Rev.1 (231.0-B-4 TC synchronization and channel coding), -25C Rev.1 (232.0-B-4 TC SDLP) and -26C (232.1-B-2 COP-1). *(PDF p. 6)*
- ECSS-E-ST-50-01C, -50-03C and -50-04C (the 2008 TM/TC standards) do not appear in the November 2025 E-50 chart, whose CCSDS link-layer entries are all adoption notices. The charts give no reason for their absence. *(PDF p. 6)*
- ECSS-E-HB-50A (Communications guidelines) is listed under E-50 in the engineering handbooks chart. No handbook is listed under E-70. *(PDF p. 11)*
- The documentation series comprises ECSS-P-00C (standardization objectives, policies and organization), ECSS-D-00B (ECSS processes), ECSS-D-00-01C (drafting rules for standards), ECSS-D-00-02A (drafting rules for handbooks) and ECSS-D-00-11A (public review and handbook release criteria). *(PDF p. 12)*
