## Summary

The CCSDS **Physical Layer** book: a loose-leaf collection of individual recommendations, in the CCIR 'considering / recommends' style, for the RF and modulation systems of Earth stations and spacecraft. It is the basis of the [radio frequency, modulation and ranging](../ccsds/rf-and-ranging.md) page and sits below the [TM](../ccsds/tm-coding.md) and [TC](../ccsds/tc-coding-cltu.md) coding standards, which cite it normatively. Part 1 covers conventional near-Earth and deep-space missions; a Part 2 for data relay satellites is foreseen. Each recommendation is tagged **Category A** (missions closer than 2 million km) or **Category B** (deep space), or applies to both when it has no suffix.

Section 2 (technical) covers six areas:

* Earth-to-space RF: phase modulation on a residual carrier, sweep ranges and rates, multiple uplink carriers;
* telecommand: 8 or 16 kHz PSK sine-wave subcarriers with NRZ-L/M, low rates of 4000/2^n symbol/s, medium-rate PCM/PM/bi-phase-L, and BPSK up to 2.048 Msymbol/s;
* space-to-Earth RF;
* telemetry: PCM formats, symbol transition density, QPSK conventions, modulator imbalances, spurious emissions, and the high-rate bandwidth-efficient modulations (GMSK, filtered OQPSK, SRRC-8PSK, 4D-8PSK TCM, APSK) for the 2, 8, 26 and 32 GHz bands;
* radio metric: group delay calibration and stability, PN ranging, telemetry ranging, Delta-DOR;
* spacecraft: transponder turnaround ratios such as 221/240 and 749/880, plus new ratios for multiple spacecraft per aperture and lunar inter-satellite links.

Section 3 (policy) covers frequency utilisation (including the Category B channel plan and multiple spacecraft per aperture), uplink power limits, ranging waveforms, charged-particle calibration, the spacecraft and Earth station compatibility tests, and interference. Section 4 (procedural) gives the link Design Control Table with its statistics and the occupied-bandwidth algorithms, and section 5 the terminology and glossary.


## Key points

- Issue 32 updates recommendations 2.4.18 and 2.4.23 and adds 2.6.13 and 2.6.15. *(PDF p. 11)*
- Part 1 targets conventional near-Earth and deep-space missions with moderate communications requirements; Part 2 is to cover data relay satellites. *(PDF p. 5)*
- Newer recommendations follow the nomenclature of the CCSDS Publications Manual (A20.0-Y-4); older ones keep the conventions of the original Blue Book. *(PDF p. 13)*
- Recommendations are grouped as technical (section 2), policy (section 3) and procedural (section 4); a page label such as 2.5.3A-1 encodes section, subsection, position, mission category and page. *(PDF p. 26)*
- Category A missions are at an altitude below 2.0 x 10^6 km and Category B missions at or beyond it, matching the Deep Space boundary set by WARC ORB-88 and effective on 16 March 1990; a recommendation without an A or B suffix applies to both. *(PDF p. 27)*
- Agencies shall support phase modulation with a residual carrier on Earth-to-space links. *(PDF p. 36)*
- Telecommand subcarriers are PSK-modulated sine waves of 8 kHz or 16 kHz. *(PDF p. 49)*
- Telecommand data use NRZ-L or NRZ-M on PSK subcarriers, with attention to the symbol transition density. *(PDF p. 50)*
- Low-rate telecommand coded symbol rates are 4000/2^n symbol/s (n = 0 to 9), with symbol and subcarrier transitions coinciding; 4000 symbol/s only with the 16 kHz subcarrier. *(PDF p. 51)*
- Medium-rate telecommand uses PCM/PM/bi-phase-L directly on the carrier at 4000 x 2^n coded symbol/s (n = 1 to 6). *(PDF p. 54)*
- When a residual-carrier system is insufficient, telecommand uses BPSK up to 2.048 coded Msymbol/s at 1000 x 2^n coded symbol/s (n = 0 to 11). *(PDF p. 55)*
- Space-to-Earth links: at most 64 consecutive identical coded symbols; at least 125 (Category A) or 275 (Category B) transitions per 1000 coded symbols; pseudo-randomizer use as in the TM Synchronization and Channel Coding standard. *(PDF p. 76)*
- Above 2 coded Msymbol/s, Category A space research downlinks use GMSK (BTs = 0.25, precoded), baseband-filtered OQPSK or SRRC-8PSK to meet the SFCG spectrum mask; SFCG limits the 8450-8500 MHz band to 10 MHz per mission. *(PDF p. 99)*
- EES 8 GHz high-rate downlinks use SRRC-filtered 4D-8PSK TCM, QPSK, OQPSK, 8PSK, 16/32/64APSK or filtered OQPSK to meet the SFCG mask and protect the adjacent deep-space band above 8400 MHz. *(PDF p. 110)*
- Telemetry ranging records the uplink PN range-code phase and the frame counter of a TM or AOS frame on board; it can reach 1 m standard deviation at 200 ksymbol/s or more and costs up to 4 percent of telemetry throughput. *(PDF p. 165)*
- The transponder turnaround frequency ratio for the 2025-2120/2200-2300 MHz bands is 221/240, needed only for missions requiring cross support and coherent two-way links. *(PDF p. 184)*
- The 7145-7235/8400-8500 MHz ratio is 749/880. *(PDF p. 185)*
- New in issue 32: flexible turnaround ratios with '749' in the numerator for multiple spacecraft per aperture in the 7/8 GHz bands (2.6.13). *(PDF p. 199)*
- New in issue 32: 2407/2800 and 2407/2816 ratios for coherent 23.15-23.55 GHz forward and 27.0-27.5 GHz return inter-satellite links, for example lunar orbit-surface proximity links (2.6.15). *(PDF p. 202)*
- Category B missions in the 2, 7, 8, 32 and 34 GHz bands select frequencies from the channel frequency plan of table 3.1.6B-1, coordinated through an organisation such as the SFCG. *(PDF p. 219)*
- All relevant spacecraft-Earth station compatibility tests of table 3.5.1-1 shall be performed before cross support. *(PDF p. 236)*
- The uniform link Design Control Table is used to exchange link budgets between agencies, with 3-sigma tolerances for telecommand and 2-sigma for all other systems. *(PDF p. 249)*
- Uncertain link parameters are modelled with uniform, triangular or truncated Gaussian probability density functions, with default assignments per budget line. *(PDF p. 282)*

Placeholder pages exist for reserved numbers (for example 'RESERVED for RECOMMENDATION 401 (2.2.1)'), and several recommendations are marked DELETED (for example 2.1.8A, 2.1.8B, 2.4.4, 2.4.5, 3.1.3A, 3.1.5B, 3.3.3A, 3.3.4, 2.6.12). The table of contents prints '2.1.4.B' for 2.1.4B. Only five formal reference entries exist (three in the front matter and two in 4.1.4); other documents (CCSDS coding books, 413.x and 414.1 ranging documents, SFCG recommendations, ITU-R SA.509) are cited inline and recorded as 'mentioned'. The Mission Categories figure (1.5-1) and most plots are not extracted. PDF pages 1 and 2 are duplicate covers; the extraction has 323 PDF pages (registry: 322). No individual authors are named.
