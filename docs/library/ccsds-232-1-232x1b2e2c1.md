## Summary

The CCSDS Recommended Standard for **COP-1**, the closed-loop go-back-n retransmission procedure that gives the [TC Space Data Link Protocol](../ccsds/tc-sdlp.md) (and, since Cor. 1, [USLP](../ccsds/uslp.md)) complete, in-order, duplicate-free delivery of telecommand frame data units on each Virtual Channel. It is the specification behind the 'AD mode' and 'BD mode' of every ESA mission control system: the ground runs the **FOP-1** (Frame Operation Procedure) and the spacecraft runs the **FARM-1** (Frame Acceptance and Reporting Mechanism). The FARM-1 reports its state in the CLCW, which is returned in the OCF of [TM](../ccsds/tm-sdlp.md) or [AOS](../ccsds/aos.md) frames.

Section 2 explains the concept. Type-AD frames carry sequence-controlled data numbered N(S); Type-BC frames carry the Unlock and Set V(R) control commands; Type-BD frames carry expedited data that is sent once. The section also introduces the key variables V(S), V(R), N(R) and N(S), the Wait flow control and the Lockout mechanism. Section 3 defines the interfaces between COP-1 and the higher procedures and frame generation and reception procedures at each end. Section 4 defines the directive request and notification, asynchronous notification and transfer notification primitives (Initiate AD Service variants, Terminate, Resume, Set V(S), Set FOP sliding window, Set T1_Initial, Set Transmission_Limit, Set Timeout_Type and others).

Section 5 specifies FOP-1 (Revision B). It covers its variables (V(S), NN(R), the Sent_Queue and Wait_Queue, timers, Transmission_Count, Suspend_State and so on), its actions, and the state table over six states: Active, Retransmit without Wait, Retransmit with Wait, Initializing without BC Frame, Initializing with BC Frame, and Initial. Section 6 specifies FARM-1 with its states Open, Wait and Lockout, the sliding window (W, PW, NW), the Type-B counter and its state table. Section 7 lists the managed parameters. Annex D records the changes from the historical Telecommand Part 2.1 recommendation.


## Key points

- Cor. 1 (April 2019) extends COP-1 to USLP and clarifies the FARM window widths depending on whether Type-AD frame retransmission is allowed. *(PDF p. 7)*
- In case of conflict with the TC SDLP or USLP books, this Recommended Standard takes precedence. *(PDF p. 10)*
- COP-1 is a go-back-n ARQ: a FOP-1 at the sender and a FARM-1 at the receiver per Virtual Channel, with acceptance status returned in CLCWs. *(PDF p. 15)*
- Valid Type-B frames are always accepted by FARM-1 and only increment the CLCW Type-B counter; Type-B frames also carry the control commands. *(PDF p. 15)*
- The AD Service ensures no FDU is lost, duplicated or delivered out of sequence on one VC; it uses Type-AD frames for data and Type-BC frames for the Unlock and Set V(R) control commands; four 'Initiate AD Service' directives exist. *(PDF p. 16)*
- Key variables: V(S) at the sender, V(R) at the receiver, N(S) in the frame header and N(R) in the CLCW; a 'Wait' mechanism gives flow control. *(PDF p. 17)*
- Directive types (Table 4-1): Initiate AD Service without CLCW check, with CLCW check, with Unlock, or with Set V(R); Terminate AD Service; Resume AD Service; and the setup directives Set V(S) to V*(S), Set FOP_Sliding_Window_Width, Set T1_Initial, Set Transmission_Limit and Set Timeout_Type. *(PDF p. 28)*
- BD (Expedited) frames are sent once with no delivery guarantee, typically for recovery or when a higher layer retransmits; 'Expedited' does not mean faster, and AD mode is recommended when a reliable link exists. *(PDF p. 17)*
- Systematic retransmission can optionally be provided outside COP-1 for Type-AD or Type-BC frames and has no effect on FOP-1 behaviour. *(PDF p. 41)*
- FOP-1 states: Active (S1), Retransmit without Wait (S2), Retransmit with Wait (S3), Initializing without BC Frame (S4), Initializing with BC Frame (S5) and Initial (S6). *(PDF p. 37)*
- The FOP-1 specified here is Revision B (FOP-1B) from issue 2 of the historical Command Operation Procedures; Revision A came from issue 1. *(PDF p. 49)*
- FARM-1 states: Open (S1), Wait (S2) and Lockout (S3). *(PDF p. 61)*
- FOP-1 managed parameters: T1_Initial, Transmission_Limit, FOP_Sliding_Window_Width K (1 to 255, K &lt;= PW) and Timeout_Type (0 or 1). *(PDF p. 72)*
- FARM-1 managed parameters: W (1 to 256), PW (1 to 256), NW (0 to 127) and the CLCW reporting period; with the 6.1.8.3.1 operation W is an even number from 2 to 254 and PW = NW = W/2, while the 6.1.8.3.2 operation allows PW &gt; NW down to NW = 0, PW = W. *(PDF p. 73)*
- Terminology changed from the historical book: 'Command Link Control Word' became 'Communications Link Control Word' and 'Command Operation Procedure' became 'Communications Operation Procedure'. *(PDF p. 79)*
