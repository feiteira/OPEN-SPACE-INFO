## Summary

The CCSDS Recommended Standard that defines the **Return All Frames (RAF)** service of the [Space Link Extension](../ccsds/sle/index.md). RAF is the SLE transfer service a ground station uses to deliver every telemetry frame of the selected quality from one space link physical channel to a mission user. The frames can be [TM](../ccsds/tm-sdlp.md), [AOS](../ccsds/aos.md) or, new in issue 5, [USLP](../ccsds/uslp.md) transfer frames. Each frame travels in an SLE-SDU annotated with Earth receive time, antenna ID, data-link continuity, delivered frame quality ('good' or 'erred', judged from Reed-Solomon/LDPC decoding or the FECF) and an optional private annotation.

Section 2 places RAF in the SLE Reference Model. It explains how service production (the Return Space Link Processing functional group) differs from service provision, how service management sets user- or provider-initiated binding and the delivery mode (timely online, complete online or offline), and how buffering works (transfer buffer, online and offline frame buffers). It also covers security concerns. Section 3 specifies each operation with its parameter table, effects and diagnostics: RAF-BIND (with version negotiation; this issue is version '6'), UNBIND, START (time window and requested frame quality), STOP, TRANSFER-DATA, SYNC-NOTIFY (loss of frame sync, production status change, data discarded due to excessive backlog, end of data), SCHEDULE-STATUS-REPORT, STATUS-REPORT, GET-PARAMETER and PEER-ABORT, together with access control and the 'all'/'bind'/'none' authentication levels. Section 4 gives the provider state machine (unbound, ready, active). The annexes contain the normative ASN.1 modules, the production-status model (running, interrupted, halted) and a conformance matrix. This is the service through which ground stations hand frames to control systems such as [SCOS-2000 via NCTRS/NIS](../scos2000/nctrs-nis.md).


## Key points

- Issue 5 (July 2023) adds support for USLP Transfer Frames; issues B-1 (2002), B-2 (2004), B-3 (2010) and B-4 (2016) are superseded; Editorial Change 1 (October 2024) only corrects a figure font. *(PDF p. 6)*
- The RAF service delivers to a mission user all telemetry frames of the selected frame quality from one space link physical channel. *(PDF p. 9)*
- The underlying communications service must deliver invocations and returns in sequence, completely and with integrity, without duplication, with flow control and with notification of disruption. *(PDF p. 9)*
- Multiplexing Reed-Solomon coded and uncoded frames on one physical channel, allowed by the historical AOS architecture (701.0-B-3), is no longer allowed by the recommendations in force. *(PDF p. 10)*
- A telemetry frame is a TM, AOS or USLP Transfer Frame; each acquired frame is wrapped in an SLE-SDU with annotation such as the ERT. *(PDF p. 23)*
- Delivery modes are timely online (controlled discarding allowed after a latency limit), complete online and offline; each service instance supports exactly one mode and either user or provider initiation, both set by service management. *(PDF p. 25)*
- For a 'good' Reed-Solomon encoded frame the RS check symbols are removed before delivery; an 'erred' RS frame is delivered complete with check symbols. *(PDF p. 27)*
- RAF operations: BIND and UNBIND (user or provider), START, STOP, SCHEDULE-STATUS-REPORT and GET-PARAMETER (user, confirmed), TRANSFER-DATA, SYNC-NOTIFY and STATUS-REPORT (provider, unconfirmed), and PEER-ABORT. *(PDF p. 32)*
- The version-number value for the RAF service defined by this issue is '6'; a responder may propose a lower version during BIND negotiation. *(PDF p. 63)*
- requested-frame-quality in RAF-START is one of 'good frames only', 'erred frames only' or 'all frames'. *(PDF p. 73)*
- RAF-TRANSFER-DATA carries invoker-credentials, earth-receive-time, antenna-ID, data-link-continuity, delivered-frame-quality, private-annotation and data. *(PDF p. 77)*
- earth-receive-time is the UTC time at which the leading edge of the first bit of the Attached Sync Marker preceding the frame was at the phase centre of the receiving antenna. *(PDF p. 78)*
- data-link-continuity is -1 for the first frame after the start of production, 0 for a direct successor, and a positive estimate of missed frames otherwise (1 if no better estimate). *(PDF p. 79)*
- RAF-SYNC-NOTIFY notification types: 'loss of frame synchronization', 'production status change' (running, halted or interrupted), 'data discarded due to excessive backlog' and 'end of data'. *(PDF p. 83)*
- GET-PARAMETER parameters include delivery-mode, latency-limit, minimum-reporting-cycle, permitted-frame-quality-set, reporting-cycle, requested-frame-quality, return-timeout-period and transfer-buffer-size. *(PDF p. 95)*
- Annex A defines the ASN.1 modules CCSDS-SLE-TRANSFER-SERVICE-COMMON-TYPES, -BIND-TYPES, -COMMON-PDUS, -SERVICE-INSTANCE-ID, -RAF-STRUCTURES, -RAF-INCOMING-PDUS and -RAF-OUTGOING-PDUS. *(PDF p. 115)*
