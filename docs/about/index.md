# About this wiki

**OPEN-SPACE-INFO** is an independent, open wiki that explains how spacecraft and their ground
segments exchange data. It covers the CCSDS protocols, the ECSS Packet Utilization Standard,
ESA/ESOC's SCOS-2000 mission control system and its ancillary systems, and the SCOS-2000 Mission
Information Base (MIB), illustrated with the real, publicly posted Solar Orbiter MIB.

!!! abstract "Sourced from public documents"
    **All information on this site was retrieved from sources publicly available online.** Every page
    cites the documents it relies on, and [Sources & copies](sources-and-copies.md) links back to each
    original with the time we copied it.

## Who it is for

Engineers, students and enthusiasts who want to understand an operational ESA-style ground segment
end to end, from a bit in an on-board packet to a value on an operator's display. The wiki also
suits anyone who needs to read a real MIB.

## How to use it

* Start with [How it all fits together](../overview/index.md), then follow the
  [downlink](../overview/downlink.md) and [uplink](../overview/uplink.md) walkthroughs.
* Each standard has its own page with field-by-field diagrams, under [CCSDS](../ccsds/index.md) and
  [ECSS & PUS](../ecss/index.md).
* The [MIB](../mib/index.md) section explains the database, and the [MIB Explorer](../explorer/index.md)
  shows every item of the Solar Orbiter MIB (2025 and 2020 releases) and of the SWA and STIX instrument databases.
* The [Library](../library/index.md) and the [reference catalogue](../references/index.md) let you
  follow any claim back to its source document.

## Contributing

Corrections and additions are welcome as issues or pull requests on
[GitHub](https://github.com/feiteira/OPEN-SPACE-INFO). Every factual statement must carry a citation
(clause and PDF page), as described in the
[authoring guide](https://github.com/feiteira/OPEN-SPACE-INFO/blob/main/AUTHORING.md). Read how the
pages were produced in [Methodology](methodology.md).
